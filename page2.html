Pasta, pizza, carne (meat) and pesce (fish) are the standard fixtures on restaurant menus up and down Italy.
As a general rule, for more genuine, tastier versions of ubiquitous dishes such as pizza or spaghetti carbonara, order them in their places of origin. Here, it’s likely they’ll be prepared to exacting standards, using age-old recipes.

Some of the most popular Italian Cuisine:

  • Pizza
  • Spaghetti Carbonara
  • Gelato
  • Lasagne al Forno
